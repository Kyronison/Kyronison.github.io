Origins of Giants Pipeline

Origins of Giants is an English-language YouTube channel with documentaries about how well-known companies began and how they work.

Origins of Giants Pipeline is the channel's internal production tool. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the channel's own videos and Shorts, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules, post the channel's own comments, and read the channel's own analytics.

The tool is used by the channel owner only. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not access any other user's account.
